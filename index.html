GOVERNMENT OF KERALA

The Government of Kerala was formed on 1 November 1956, following the reorganisation of states on a linguistic basis. Kerala introduced a three-tier Panchayati Raj system to strengthen local self-government and promote people's participation in development. The system consists of Grama Panchayats at the village level, Block Panchayats at the intermediate level, and District Panchayats at the district level. Grama Panchayats were formed to administer local areas and address the basic needs and development requirements of local communities. Over time, Panchayats have become an important part of Kerala's decentralised governance and rural development system.
